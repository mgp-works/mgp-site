Gyul Hero

Project overview

Gyul Hero is a social impact campaign that reframes "ugly" tangerines — misshapen fruit discarded for cosmetic reasons — as a smart, worthwhile purchase. The project centered on a crowdfunding campaign for a DIY tangerine marmalade kit, supported by character branding, social content, and packaging design.

Context and problem

Jeju farms often discard or discount "ugly" tangerines for cosmetic reasons alone, even though taste and quality are unaffected — hurting farmer income. The goal was to position these tangerines as a value-driven choice, not a lesser product.

My role and responsibilities

As sole designer: mascot/character branding, promotional content (carousel posts, photo, video), crowdfunding campaign page, and packaging/print materials (leaflet, recipe book, stickers, postcards). Also contributed to planning and marketing strategy.

Design process and key decisions

  • Mascot branding: Designed three tangerine characters with distinct personalities to make the product feel approachable rather than "discounted."
  • Staged validation: Tested reception with a 2020 trial (green tangerine syrup and marmalade tasting) before launching the full 2021 crowdfunding kit.
  • Ongoing content: Ran weekly Instagram carousel posts (farmer interviews, recipes) to sustain awareness beyond the funding window.

Final solution

  • Brand identity: mascots, logo, color palette
  • DIY marmalade kit (tangerines, jar, sugar, spoon, leaflet, recipe book, stickers, postcards), made with an eco-friendly farm in Jeju
  • Content channels: Instagram, YouTube recipe video
  • Wadiz crowdfunding campaign page

Results

Wadiz campaign closed December 2021 at 126% of its funding goal.
