Project overview

Prelude helps pregnant users make informed food choices by analyzing meal photos and providing personalized safety guidance based on medical research and health standards. After launch, we surveyed pregnant women to understand their experiences with food. Based on these insights, we redesigned the results page to reduce uncertainty and make guidance easier to understand and act on.
