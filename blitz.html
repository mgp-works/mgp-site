Blitz

Problem

Hackathon participants had no single place to research events — information was scattered across sites, reviews were mixed across years with no way to compare, and tracking application deadlines across platforms was hard to manage.

Research

I ran interviews with 4 participants to test 3 hypotheses formed from my own hackathon experience. Two were confirmed with a different root cause than expected: fatigue came from duplicate/expired listings and missing filters (not too many sites), and users compared reviews across multiple years, not just the latest ones. A 4th need — team building — emerged directly from interviews: 75% of participants were open to teaming with new people but lacked a way to judge compatibility.

Key decisions

  • Added a team-matching feature (by tech stack and collaboration style) with in-app chat, based on the team-building need surfaced in interviews.
  • Redesigned the main discovery flow after usability testing showed that requiring interest registration before recommendations caused onboarding drop-off. The fix: show the full list and themed curation together immediately, with no registration step required.

Results

  • SUS score improved by 13 points after the flow redesign
  • Steps to reach relevant hackathons reduced from 3 → 1
  • Interviews confirmed 3 hypotheses and surfaced 1 new core need
