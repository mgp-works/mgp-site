Prelude

Overview

Prelude analyzes a meal photo with AI and flags ingredients pregnant users should watch for, using trusted health sources and their own health profile.

Problem

The original result screen listed nutrients and raw source URLs. Users couldn't quickly tell if a meal was safe, and the sourcing looked unverified, which undermined trust.

Research

A user survey showed three things: users default to avoiding any risk, however small; they cared more about health impact than nutrient values; and unclear sourcing made them distrust the answer.

Solution

  • Made the danger/caution/safe summary at the top of the screen easier to read at a glance by adding a visual chart.
  • Replaced raw URLs with named institutions (e.g., "World Health Organization (who.int)").
  • Rewrote the copy to lead with health impact in a calm, reassuring tone rather than alarmist language.

Loading time improvement

Separately, I designed a fix for a related problem: the AI's food recognition took over two minutes and gave users no way to correct a wrong match. I proposed a selection step that shows the top 3 visual matches before running the full analysis, so users could confirm the right food first — expected to cut the wait down to just a few seconds.
