Encrebel

Project overview

ENCREBEL is a branding and packaging design project for a tattoo care and ink brand. The name combines “encre,” the French word for ink, with “rebel,” reflecting the brand’s support for people who express themselves through tattoos despite social stigma and conventional beauty standards. In Korea, tattoos remain socially controversial and are still legally restricted when performed outside the medical system. ENCREBEL positions tattoo culture not as an act of defiance for its own sake, but as a personal form of identity, beauty, and self-expression.

Brand concept

The brand was built around three core visual keywords: gothic blackletter, black ink, and delicacy. These keywords connect directly to the visual language commonly associated with tattoo culture while giving the brand a refined and contemporary identity. Rather than using an aggressive or overly rebellious tone, ENCREBEL balances boldness with sensitivity. The brand communicates confidence, individuality, and care through a restrained black-and-white visual system and detailed botanical artwork.

Logo design

The logo incorporates subtle blackletter-inspired details, referencing classic tattoo lettering without making the identity feel outdated or overly decorative. The sharp, structured letterforms express the rebellious attitude of the brand, while the clean composition keeps the logo suitable for modern packaging and digital use. Black was selected as the primary brand color to represent tattoo ink, permanence, and visual clarity. This also allows the detailed artwork and product information to stand out across different applications.

Brand artwork

To express the delicate side of tattoo culture, custom botanical artwork was created for the brand system. The illustration features peonies, roses, and leaf elements, referencing some of the most commonly tattooed floral motifs. The floral direction also supports the brand’s values of eco-friendliness, sustainability, cruelty-free production, and vegan formulation. By combining tattoo symbolism with natural forms, the artwork creates a softer and more thoughtful impression of a tattoo-related brand.

Packaging system

The packaging system includes tattoo aftercare products, tattoo ink, a gift bag, and a guide leaflet. Each product category was designed with a slightly different visual approach while maintaining a consistent brand identity. The aftercare line uses the botanical artwork as a main visual element to emphasize care, delicacy, and skin recovery. In contrast, the tattoo ink line follows a more minimal design direction, allowing the color of the ink itself to become the focal point.

Visual direction

ENCREBEL’s visual identity combines tattoo-inspired graphic elements with a refined editorial layout. The use of black, white, and detailed line-based artwork creates a strong but elegant brand image. The final direction presents tattoo culture as intentional, expressive, and aesthetically sophisticated, challenging the negative perception often attached to tattoos while remaining visually accessible as a beauty and lifestyle brand.
