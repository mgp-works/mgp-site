Encrebel

Project overview

Encrebel is a branding and packaging project for a tattoo care and ink brand. The name combines the French word encre ("ink") with rebel, representing people who express themselves through tattoos despite social stigma. Rather than focusing on rebellion, the brand presents tattoos as a form of identity, beauty, and self-expression.

Brand concept

The brand is built around three visual keywords: blackletter, black ink, and delicacy. It combines tattoo-inspired visuals with a refined aesthetic, balancing boldness with sensitivity through a restrained black-and-white palette and botanical artwork.

Logo design

The logo incorporates subtle blackletter-inspired details that reference classic tattoo lettering while maintaining a modern appearance. Black represents tattoo ink and permanence, creating a clear identity that works consistently across packaging and digital applications.

Brand artwork

Custom botanical illustrations featuring peonies, roses, and leaves soften the brand's visual identity while referencing popular tattoo motifs. The artwork also reflects the brand's values of sustainability, cruelty-free production, and vegan formulation.

Packaging system

The packaging system includes tattoo aftercare products, tattoo ink, a gift bag, and a guide leaflet. While each category has its own visual emphasis, all products share a consistent brand identity. The aftercare line highlights botanical artwork, while the ink line adopts a more minimal approach that lets the ink color stand out.
